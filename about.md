# About

Hello there! My name is Fırat Gelbal. I love telling stories, and I’m exploring how to tell them better with AI. I back my stories with statistical rigor, beautiful visualizations, and thoughtful design. On this website you see my essays, visuals, code, and listening notes.


I’m a data scientist at [Automattic](https://automattic.com/), where I work on experiments and product analytics for WordPress.com. I also lead the editorial team at [data.blog](https://data.blog/), Automattic’s public data blog. Before Automattic, I led analytics at balena, worked at Cimpress, did data engineering and game design at DeNA, and co-founded a recommender-systems startup in İstanbul.


Working remotely helps with traveling and living in different parts of the world. Nowadays I’m in Barcelona, Ankara, and İstanbul.
