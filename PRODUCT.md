# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Readers, peers, collaborators, and prospective colleagues exploring Fırat Gelbal's essays, visual work, data projects, and code. They may arrive through an individual article, a topic page, a shared social link, a search engine, an RSS reader, or an AI agent.

## Product Purpose

Present Fırat's work and thinking as a durable, personal body of work. The site should make substantial writing comfortable to read, make projects and topics easy to discover, and serve both human and machine readers without changing the character of the content.

## Brand Personality

Curious, precise, personal.

## Anti-references

- Generic portfolio templates that reduce the work to a résumé or a grid of interchangeable cards.
- Marketing-heavy presentation that overstates claims or competes with the writing.
- Decorative complexity that obscures navigation, reading, or the distinct character of the projects.
- Minimalism that achieves visual quiet by sacrificing readable type, keyboard access, or clear focus.

## Design Principles

1. Put the work first: typography, navigation, and imagery support the essays and projects.
2. Prefer evidence over ornament: show real writing, visuals, and project outputs.
3. Keep discovery intentional: latest work, favourites, categories, topics, search, and feeds should agree.
4. Respect identity and language: preserve Unicode text accurately, especially Turkish characters.
5. Welcome every reader: human pages, machine-readable alternatives, and metadata should be equally reliable.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 Level AA. All primary journeys must work with keyboard navigation and screen readers, focus must remain visible and predictable, interactive targets must remain usable on small screens, motion must respect reduced-motion preferences, and the layout must tolerate text zoom and long or multilingual content.
